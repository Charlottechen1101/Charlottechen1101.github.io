Hi to whoever visits this website, I'm Charlotte. I'm heading to the third year studying in NTHU, majoring in foreign language and literature. At the same time, I find it hard to figure things out, or to document those phenomenal moments in my life. I mean, in this time of the world, being a 21 year old girl is hard and lost (I know it’s pretty cliche but it’s true). How to live a joyful life, what to do, what to study, the practice of love, and more on. I guess this website is a place for me to record those things.

My college life in the mountaineering club in NTHU has been a great adventure, and it’s going to be more fun!
