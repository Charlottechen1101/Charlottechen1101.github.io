南北大武

繁中

20240227-3/2

I have yearned to climb high mountains for so long-wanting to roll around on the meadows, to watch the blazing orange sun peek over the ridges, and to leave everything behind to be a free spirit, entirely present in the moment. The feeling this trip has given to me bis extremely intense and real. I often recall the whole journy and find it hard to articulate or summarize simply. Though diluted and worn by time, they made me fall in love with the mountains, and with this feeling of a trembling soul.

Both my physical endurance and my horizons reached a whole new level during this hike. Our physical energy was heavily drained day after day, but every gasp and every adrenaline-rushing moment along the way accumulated in these four days is where its charm lies! I became a part of the ridgelines, the flowers, the fallen logs, and the moss. Only the present required thought. There was no past and no future.

On the first day, we hiked up to 2000 meters with our heavy backpacks. Upon reaching the campsite, we pushed on for a single-day ascent of 北大武山. Sweat slid down my soaked bangs and into my eyes, it stung, and I was panting heavily. Before giving up, I had to ask myself, Have I given it my all? There are multiple peaks to the summit, which make the hike more tiring mentally. I wondered, how does the summit of the Sacred Mountain of the Southern Taiwan actually look like? She was magnificent and serene. With our own two feet, we finally witnessed the sea of clouds we had fantasized about for so long. Everything felt so fresh and beautiful.

On the second day, we set off for 南大武山, weaving through dense groves of arrow bamboo. I love the feeling of walking on a knife-edge ridge, it's like standing at the highest point of this vast and uncertain world, eagerly rushing ahead to see everything. This anticipation felt long, distant, and heavy. 首中 always loved to gossip, 宇謙 would just give him a long smile to make him drop the questioning, and I would just watch the two of them with a grin. Walking along that narrow ridgeline, gazing at the sea of clouds, the mountains around us, it was such a visual feast. Finding the path, navigating, labelling one sign, and holding the silvergrass to carefully cross a dangerous ridge. I was always terrified of misstepping and falling. Fortunately, the grass was dense and sturdy, and with 好笨's gentle guidance, I was less afraid. Still, one had to be careful not to grab thorny plants, casual trust can bring piercing pain. Chatting as we went, we smoothly crossed these terrains one by one. Even so, it took us much longer than expected, and we eventually had to make an emergency bivouac at 彎刀營地.

The green of the ridge starting out was a mosaic of different shades. Upon a grayish-brown surface lay a variety of green like gray green, scallion green, and then deep ink-green grass. With just a shift in perspective, the ridge transformed into a completely different look. As we walked, we summited peak after peak. Whenever I looked back, beyond marveling at the strength of my own feet, I appreciated the embrace between the ridges and the sea of clouds. Looking down along the mountain's spine, they looked like they were wearing skirts, their lower hemlines wrapped by a dense, thick, yet light and ethereal sea of clouds. Since there weren't many mountains along this route, we no longer had to look up at the clouds. Often, looking out as far as the eye could see, there was a vast expanse of clouds, the sea of clouds, the grand canopy above, and the limitless ocean shared a blurred and ambiguous boundary.Their different shades of blue bleeding into one another. Every sea of clouds we witnessed possessed a unique beauty. The most breathtaking moment was at sunset, when the blazing orange sun slowly sank into the sea of clouds, and the rosy dusk painted the entire world into another form of perfection. The clouds in the sky, the sea of clouds at our feet, the grass on the ridges, and the branches around us all underwent subtle yet direct shifts in radiance at this very moment. Before the dark night arrived, I greedily wanted to grasp everything within my sight to feed my impoverished, narrow soul. The temperature dropped lower and lower, I only wanted to curl up in my sleeping bag and dream a dream just like today's.

The third day was just as fulfilling, and it was also the most exciting day. 壬煜 had just told me yesterday that the highlight of today would be the most terrifying part, so I didn't dare slack off for a single second. Not long after starting our ascent, as we came up onto the ridgeline, we looked out to see a vast expanse of what imagined perfection in the world should look like. I believe I held my breath at that moment. The orange-red sunrise and the seamless sea of clouds met and merged, as if setting the entire world ablaze. It was a breathtaking. Everyone's face was illuminated in gold, and their hair shines with a beautiful spark. My exclamations of suprise never stopped. I think I must have said 'wow' and 'OMG' twenty times! To put it extremely, it was a beauty so profound one could die with no regrets.

Humans are always greedy, I wanted to sit there and watch that sky all day long, but the miles still had to be covered. The path on the ridge was treacherous. While forging ahead with all our might, we admired the dawning sunlight breaking through the cloud cover beneath our feet, chasing the white mist. We could also see the distant summit of 北大武山, shrouded in a thin veil of haze. The clouds were like billowing sails, or a leisurely eagle drifting between heaven and earth, as we trod upon the ridge reflected by the rising sun and drifting clouds.

After a steep scramble to the triangulation point of 南大武山, at 11:30, we began to face the difficult terrain between 南大武山 and its forepeak. The towering appearance of the peaks seen from afar now appear right before our feet. From the knife-edge ridge to the down-climb, and then traversing to the razor ridge. Looking down at that near-vertical, barren rock wall, my heart couldn't help but leap. With a mix of thrill and dread, I stared straight at it: how were we supposed to get across? The most dangerous parts were the traverse onto the knife-edge ridge and passing the razor ridge itself. One section had absolutely no footholds, only a thin webbing strap on it, and no choice but to crawl forward. With the guidance of 蘇老師, and perhaps out of fear from the previous terrain, I chose to cross first. I crossed with absolute focus and bated breath, as if a single second of other thoughts or a moment of fear would make my body feel heavier. The heavy backpack and the exposed ridge made it all more terrifying and difficult. I took deep breaths, my fingers were still trembling, and my cheeks stung from the scorching heat of the sun. At this very moment, I vividly felt the vividness and fragility of life, death was so close. Looking back at the distant 首中, I cautiously and awkwardly overcame the obstacles like someone riding an elephant, conquering each challenge one by one. "I'm across! I'll wait for you guys in a safe spot!" I rushed to a shaded area to let my racing heart and burning body cool down. I hoped to see and hear them soon, but little did I know this wait would be a nerve-wracking hour. The whole team didn't cross safely until 13:40. Afterward, we took a short rest in the shade of the forepeak and decided to split the team. 宇謙 and 首中 set off ahead to 佳興營地 to get water first. The other team: myself, 佩璇, 好笨, and 壬煜 set off at 14:30.

From the initial route-finding, setting ropes, to crossing the terrain, I was amazed and wondered why 好笨 could always bring such peace of mind, always crossing the terrain without changing expression before making a smile. 宇謙's eyes always sparkled when walking, and he was always so reliable. 壬煜, as the leader and guide, showed such easiness, gentleness, and lightness in his steps. Even though 首中 was terrified to the point of his legs shaking, he still acted as the pioneer to set up the ropes. They all possessed such ease and capability! I still remember 好笨 asking me along the way if I wanted to take the leader exam in our club. Back then, it felt so far away, but at this exact moment of writing finishing this piece, I am already a leader. Though I didn't listen to 好笨's advice to drink more boba while I was still young XD.

Our two teams only reunited six hours later. From the steep descent through the silvergrass and the scorching sun to the logging road. The forest illuminated only by headlamps was daunting. The anxiety of rushing in the dark vanished the seconf I saw them. I wanted to give them a hug. The moonlight pierced through the treetops, casting shadows on the logging road as we gulped down that precious water, happily sharing stories of the fireflies we saw and the sky full of stars.

At 21:40, we arrived at 佳興山莊. Dragging our exhausted bodies, our hearts were full, overflowing with gratitude for our teammates. The next day, we leisurely made breakfast, playing with the pancake for a while, and explored the surroundings of the campsite. The ferns near 佳興山莊 mirrored the sunlight, with dewdrops adorning the lace-like edges of the leaves. Occasionally, tiny purple flowers would peek out, and colorful butterflies danced a waltz above. We didn't set off until 10:00, using our blistered and aching feet to get to our destination. 好笨 and I developed "low-altitude sickness", our fingers swelled up, feeling a bit tight and uncomfortable. It seems the golden rule at this point was to immediately gain altitude! My body was screaming its discomfort at descending and approaching the city. This feeling thus accompanied me down the mountain and all the way home, fading away along with the muscle soreness.
